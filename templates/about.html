Mission & Architecture • Access to Justice & Equitable Contracting

Making Legal Documents Understandable for Everyone

ClauseWise was engineered for a GenAI hackathon challenge focused on legal assistance and access to justice. Our goal: empower founders, freelancers, consumers, and small business owners to comprehend complex agreements before signing.

The Problem

Commercial agreements and residential leases are intentionally dense, filled with archaic legalese, and rarely read in full. When individuals cannot afford $450-$750/hr billable legal rates just for an initial read-through, they sign under profound informational asymmetry.

"Over 85% of non-disclosure and service agreements are signed without understanding indemnification exposure or automatic renewal lock-ins."

The Solution

ClauseWise acts as an intelligent pre-review lens. It segments agreements clause by clause, translates dense prose into plain English, flags atypical exposure terms, and formulates precise questions for your consultation.

✓ Drastically cuts attorney billable review time by walking into meetings prepared with specific clause queries.

What ClauseWise Does

Three core pillars built to transform legalese into actionable insight

1. Deep Clause Analysis

Parses PDFs, Word docs, and plain text. Labels every provision into 11 commercial standards, benchmarks liability terms, and assigns clear risk levels.

What ClauseWise Is NOT

Clear ethical boundaries and regulatory compliance

ClauseWise does not provide legal advice, does not provide an attorney-client relationship, and does not tell you whether or not to sign an agreement. It is an educational understanding and consultation preparation engine. When you consult an attorney, you walk in with high clarity rather than a blank page.

How It Works

Five-stage automated pipeline designed for speed, privacy, and precision

1

Ephemeral Document Ingestion

Upload your agreement in PDF, DOCX, or TXT format. Text is extracted securely in memory with client-side file size and MIME validation.

2

NLP Clause Segmentation & Category Tagging

Every article and numbered section is extracted into standalone provisions and labeled across standard commercial legal categories.

3

Exposure Pattern & Risk Auditing

Provisions are cross-checked against known high-exposure patterns: uncapped indemnities, automatic renewal traps, unilateral IP assignment, and non-compete clauses.

4

Plain-English Translation & Question Synthesis

Multi-tiered LLM prompts translate archaic terms into clear everyday English and compile targeted discussion points for your legal counsel.

5

Immediate Ephemeral Memory Wipe

Zero data persistence. The moment your analysis or comparison payload is returned, temporary server files are wiped from the disk.
