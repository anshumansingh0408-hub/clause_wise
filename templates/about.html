About the Project

Making Legal Documents Understandable

ClauseWise was built for a GenAI hackathon challenge focused on legal assistance and access to justice, empowering everyday individuals, small business owners, and non-legal professionals to understand what they are signing.

💡

The Problem

Legal documents are dense, jargon-heavy, and often unread before signing - not because people don't care, but because understanding them typically requires paying a lawyer by the hour just to explain what a clause means. ClauseWise closes that gap for the initial understanding phase.

What ClauseWise Does

📄

Analyze

Break down any document clause-by-clause with risk flags and plain-English translations.

⚖️

Compare

See exactly what differs between two versions of a document, added terms, and which party language favors.

❓

Prepare

Get a ready-made list of targeted questions and an action checklist for your actual lawyer.

⚠️

What ClauseWise Is NOT

ClauseWise does not provide legal advice, does not tell you whether to sign something, and does not replace a licensed attorney. It is a preparation and understanding tool - the goal is that when you do talk to a lawyer, you walk in already knowing what to ask.

How It Works

  1. ①

    Upload your document

    Submit contracts or policies in PDF, DOCX, or plain text format (up to 10MB).

  2. ②

    AI extracts and categorizes every clause

    Provisions are parsed and labeled into standard commercial categories (e.g. Obligation, Liability, Termination).

  3. ③

    Each clause is checked against common risk patterns

    Heuristic rules and LLM validation identify red flags such as uncapped indemnification or strict auto-renewal windows.

  4. ④

    You get a plain-English summary and a lawyer-question checklist

    Complex legalese is converted into intuitive summaries alongside action items for your consultation.

  5. ⑤

    Documents are deleted immediately after processing

    Uploaded files are wiped from the server as soon as the analysis or comparison pipeline finishes.

🔒

Privacy Note

Uploaded documents are processed in memory and deleted immediately after analysis. We do not store, retain, or use your documents for any purpose beyond generating your results.
